# ENAM background texture — visual references for the art handoff

The current website uses a CSS-only muted metal/glass texture so the background stays low-contrast under the HUD. These links are reference material for Clara/the art team; they are not copied into the application.

## Recommended references

1. **NASA HERA — closed habitat interior and equipment**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/hera-about-hera/  
   Useful for compact spacecraft-like work areas, storage, equipment panels, workstations, and an enclosed habitat feeling. NASA describes HERA as an analog environment for isolation, confinement, and remote exploration conditions.

2. **NASA CHAPEA / Mars Dune Alpha**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/  
   Useful for a Mars-surface habitat identity and realistic material references. NASA describes Mars Dune Alpha as a habitat analog with separate living and working areas.

3. **NASA CHAPEA Mission 2 update (9 November 2026)**  
   https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/johnson/chapea-mission-2-crew-reflects-after-378-days-on-simulated-mars/  
   A recent official visual/context reference for the Mars Dune Alpha habitat and long-duration simulated Mars operations.

## Suggested treatment for the ENAM screen

- Use an interior/cabin image rather than a Mars landscape behind the HUD: the supplied wireframe shows the crew inside a habitat.
- Desaturate and darken the image; keep most of the background in steel grey, graphite, or near-black.
- Add a dark translucent overlay (roughly 55–70%, tune against the final artwork) to protect text contrast.
- Blur distant structural detail slightly; do not blur character art or interactive controls.
- Keep the four resource rails vivid and preserve their meanings: Shielding violet/metal, Food green/orange, Life Support cyan/white, Power yellow/amber.
- Treat NASA HERA and CHAPEA images as references, not final ENAM art. Confirm image rights/credit and obtain Clara's approval before shipping an external photograph in the contest build.
